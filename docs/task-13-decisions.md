## Eligibility and measurement

Only ACTIVE BANK, CASH and SAVINGS may be selected after explicit acknowledgement of immediate access by account identity. Type alone never proves accessibility; every such account requires INCLUDE or EXCLUDE, with absent decisions unresolved. Other types are excluded from starting funds; credit limits and liabilities are never funds. Archived BANK/CASH/SAVINGS require review and block completeness because canonical lifecycle closing evidence is absent. Each included account contributes once, retaining whole shared/member attribution without percentages. Unknown positions remain null, known zero remains zero.

Starting liquidity is the canonical **closing position of the selected date**. The request explicitly carries evidenceAsOf; pure core rejects a start after that date. Browser orchestration captures/displays the current Jerusalem date when a live authorized snapshot is refreshed. The browser clock is a calculation basis, not independently certified financial or server time. LEDGER includes opening anchor and current POSTED revisions through the selected date, with partial/missing opening propagated. OBSERVED uses dated authoritative evidence, never imported movement sums; an observation older than the selected date is carried/partial, following Task 10 rather than inventing an age threshold. No date-only position is labeled an intraday spendable bank balance. Complete means complete computation over registered records, never certified external statement coverage.

## Dates, bounds and movements

Display baseline day S plus 1–90 future days, with payments in `(S, S+horizon]`. S is included as an ending-position baseline, so its posted payments are not subtracted again. Minimum includes that baseline to prevent later income inflating capacity now. Pure Gregorian increment/clamping, no ambient clock or timezone shifts; overflow beyond year 9999 fails. Task 12 monthly start/end and version windows are reused. Maximum 1,000 expected occurrences and 1,000 projected actual movements; snapshot inherits all Task 10/12 collection and combined 16 MiB bounds. Overflow and invalid graph produce UNAVAILABLE, never a truncated successful result.

Current POSTED movements after S on included LEDGER accounts are considered once using their exact signed canonical account Money. Positive movements dated after evidenceAsOf are unconfirmed future receipts and have null projected cash; they block spending capacity regardless of whether their effect is income, transfer or refund. Already received historical movements after a historical S may remain actual signed cash. Financial effects are inspected for uncertainty, not used to invent direction. Pending/voided movements are not posted cash. Current revisions replace corrected history. Internal transfer sides each affect their account once; supported signed amounts cancel in one currency. Movements into excluded accounts subtract only the included outgoing side. Principal/interest/fees, refunds and investments retain canonical meanings; no extra expense-report subtraction. OBSERVED accounts cannot safely absorb future ledger movements as authoritative positions; relevant future movements block completeness rather than becoming a fabricated observed forecast.

Confirmed obligations on included liquid accounts are negative planned cash only after validating effective occurrence, original currency, account and payment evidence. A valid whole actual association already in the baseline suppresses the expectation; a future association is represented by its actual signed movement once. Partial, ambiguous or stale links prevent completeness. Unassociated known planned obligations remain expected outflows, never confirmed overdue debts. Future posted expense evidence without an explicit association is ambiguous overlap and blocks completeness. Candidates never contribute. Obligations on excluded/non-liquid accounts retain original-currency evidence and unresolved cash timing; never apply card purchase dates to bank cash.

CARD evidence is historical reconciliation, not a future settlement calendar. Registered card or loan exposure or obligations without explicit supported future settlement timing block safe-to-spend. A fresh COMPLETE zero debt position with no registered pending/future exposure or expected obligation is distinguished from unknown/nonzero exposure and is not itself a blocker. This is a canonical-record distinction, never a claim of complete external debt coverage. Stale/partial/incorrect reconciliation and unclassified financial effects propagate uncertainty. No card purchases are deducted from bank funds merely because reporting recognized consumption, and no settlement schedule is inferred from history. Loan and other excluded-account obligations remain unresolved timing rather than inferred bank debt.

## Currency, buffer and statuses

Selected reporting currency and explicitly entered nonnegative buffer use exact Money (8 places). Zero buffer must be entered explicitly. Starting balances may use Task 10 unique same-household exact-valuation-date immutable FX, retaining original amount/measurement/FX date. Future movements and obligations in other currencies are displayed in original currency and not converted: no future/live FX, inversion, stale fallback or silent omitted exposure. Their presence blocks combined safe-to-spend. Same-currency projected totals use exact signed addition/subtraction and overflow rejection.

Candidate = minimum(baseline and every daily ending liquidity) − buffer. If complete and candidate >= 0, MODELED_AVAILABLE retains that exact amount, including known zero. If complete and negative, MODELED_SHORTFALL retains the negative candidate and positive shortfall, with no fabricated zero available amount. Incomplete evidence always yields UNAVAILABLE safe-to-spend. Forecast coverage COMPLETE/PARTIAL/UNKNOWN/UNAVAILABLE remains distinct; usable partial daily subtotals retain PARTIAL and cannot be shown as verified household liquidity. All modeled outputs are planning estimates, never guaranteed future money or payment authorization.
