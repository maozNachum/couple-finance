New additive planning tables retain stable obligation heads, immutable revisions, original evidence references, candidate rejection decisions and immutable payment-link decisions with exclusive active transaction claims. All references are household scoped; durable members, accounts, revisions and allocations have restrictive composite foreign keys. Private complete-payload receipts and minimal audit accompany one narrow atomic command. Auth user/session/member checks and household/account lock ordering precede every receipt replay. No client DML or persistent browser storage.

Supported obligation scope is explicitly negative EXPENSE allocations only. Positive EXPENSE refunds, principal, transfers, investments, income and unknown effects remain distinct and excluded, with diagnostics. Loan interest/fee expense allocations may be explicitly linked independently of principal; automatic candidates require a single allocation. A card settlement payment/counterpart identified by retained reconciliation evidence is excluded even if misclassified EXPENSE; stale or unresolved reconciliation is visible. This bounded scope does not infer full debt, contractual duration or future installment balances.

Candidate algorithm v1 uses at least three current posted monthly occurrences. Groups keep household, account, exact normalized description, explicit merchant identity (including absence), currency and allocation ownership separate. Exact repeated amount and one valid occurrence per calendar month support a suggestion; variable amounts, missing months, duplicate months or inconsistent date patterns produce ambiguity. Normalization reuses Task 6 ASCII whitespace/case policy; identical descriptions alone cannot establish identity. A consistent monthly day or uniquely compatible clamped day is supporting evidence, never certainty; multiple compatible days stay ambiguous. Evidence retains transaction/revision/version/allocation IDs and provenance. Registered complete graphs do not certify external statement coverage; every suggestion retains this limitation. Rejection records the reviewed evidence key and references; revised evidence can require new review.

Only monthly schedules are supported: day 1–31, shorter months clamp to their last valid day using pure Gregorian integer arithmetic. No holiday adjustments, timezone conversion or annual/weekly inference. Inclusive start/end dates; inclusive query range bounded to 120 months and 1,000 events. Each version has an explicit effectiveFrom date. Creation is effective from startDate. Later edits/archive/cancel require a strictly later effective date, preserving earlier version occurrence windows; an old date is never silently moved to a new version. Future scheduled changes do not erase earlier decisions. Effective dates can be later than an explicit end date (for example archival after completion); no events are invented past that end. End dates are explicit, never inferred. Original currency/positive exact Money remain; no future FX conversion or summation across currencies.

An original canonical revision/allocation can justify only one candidate-origin confirmation, including concurrent approvals with different command IDs. Manual plans have no deduced contractual identity and may intentionally coexist; names/amounts do not establish duplication. Exact rejection/reference sets are suppressed; materially updated historical evidence can need new review. When reviewing such a new suggestion, users must check existing plans rather than assuming it is an additional contract.

Rejection/acceptance identity uses immutable financial references, excluding the current account metadata version. Renaming an account therefore does not resurrect unchanged reviewed evidence. Original confirmation snapshots still retain their exact account-version preconditions immutably. The obligation-level payment-decision history exposes all old occurrence/version decisions even when an explicit schedule edit removes that date from the currently generated calendar.

Explicit confirmation acknowledges planning status. Amount/schedule/name/start/end edits are versioned; account, currency, allocation ownership and original evidence remain immutable. Terminal archive/cancel retains prior windows. Manual confirmation is supported. Candidate acceptance can adjust amount/schedule but must carry reviewed current evidence. Actual matching is a separate acknowledged operation referencing the exact obligation version/date and current posted transaction/revision/allocation with expected head/link versions. Matching consumes one whole transaction exclusively, preventing contradictory matches; one occurrence has at most one active link. Same account/currency/ownership and negative EXPENSE are required. A smaller amount is PARTIAL, larger amount AMBIGUOUS, and equal amount ASSOCIATED only after confirmation. No FX matching or combining partial payments in this task. Unlink releases claims and appends history; correction is explicit unlink followed by a new link. Corrected/voided revisions make links STALE. No link is proof of external contract fulfillment. Unlinked means UNRESOLVED, never missed: coverage may be incomplete.

Snapshot bounds inherit reporting limits plus 10,000 obligation/rejection/link decisions and 16 MiB combined payload; overflow fails without truncated success. Core validates complete graph and revision continuity. Memory-only exact retries survive uncertain responses; new commands are blocked until resolution. Refresh/sign-out/revocation clears protected presentation and rejects obsolete responses. Hebrew RTL UI exposes candidates, uncertainty, manual confirmation, history, expected dates and explicit link/unlink review.

Payment option computation/rendering is deferred until explicit review of an occurrence; an option set over 1,000 fails visibly rather than displaying a truncated choice list. Currency display uses the established minor-unit formatter; persisted/planning/matching Money retains all eight supported fractional digits. An exact command retry returns its immutable original receipt even after later edits/corrections; fresh snapshots independently reevaluate the current validity of actual evidence.
