## Contract/schema compatibility, before implementation

Keep canonical transactions, allocations, immutable revisions, account/source authority, opening anchors, observed balances, FX and import evidence unchanged. Add two exact ES-only portable aliases: reconciliation contracts and domain. No dependency additions. Add forward-only reconciliation tables and invoker snapshot/command APIs; no new financial writer. Existing financial/categorization commands remain the only financial correction path.

A durable household relationship has a versioned LINK/REPLACE/UNLINK decision history. Participants reference exact account versions and transaction revisions, and explicitly label every existing allocation. TRANSFER has one payment and at most one counterpart; CARD has one bank payment, optional card settlement movement and up to 98 individual purchases with a settlement movement (99 without one) on one card; LOAN has one bank payment with explicit principal/interest/fee parts and at most one loan principal counterpart. One-sided and unequal evidence is PARTIAL, never fabricated. Repeated values remain distinct identities. Each entire movement is reserved by at most one active relationship; partial purchases cannot be reused across several payments in this bounded model. Unlink/replace is explicit and atomic.

Relationship-only decisions never alter financial fields. Incorrect allocation effects yield NEEDS_CORRECTION, retaining the proposed meaning without masking consumption. The existing authorized financial form handles corrections separately and preserves category locks. Any later transaction revision makes a retained active decision STALE; it continues reserving its participants until explicitly unlinked or replaced, preventing contradictory decisions. Category-only revisions conservatively stale the decision too. Historical evidence is immutable and survives unlinking, raw cleanup and Auth deletion.

Candidates use eligible posted current household movements, account types, explicit financial effects, bounded seven-day settlement differences, exact currency/amount and retained references. Opposite amount/date alone produces weak similarity only; typed accounting effects or exact usable references provide stronger evidence, still requiring review. Card purchase groups are explicitly selected, never guessed by subset-sum. Cross-currency confirmation requires a selected stored immutable historical rate in payment-to-counterpart direction, effective on the payment date, and no silent inversion. Conversion explicitly uses eight-place HALF_UP; unexplained differences remain PARTIAL.

Commands are bounded to 100 participants / 1,000 allocations in total / 128 KiB payload, complete server JSONB digest, household-scoped receipts, expected relationship version and participant revision/account checks. Deterministic household then sorted account locks coordinate with existing commands. Authorization is rechecked on retries before receipt recovery. Atomic history, claims, receipt and minimal audit roll back on error. No sensitive browser persistence or production deployment.

Confirmed Auth denial/missing session clears protected state. Other Auth/network failures, including rate limiting, are uncertain and retain the exact in-memory retry command. Reauthorization remains mandatory in the database on every retry. No route-outcome assumption substitutes for checking that protected evidence and retry controls disappear on confirmed sign-out/revocation.

Current Supabase [function security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/functions) and [RLS guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) were checked against the pinned local implementation; no provider/CLI/library upgrade was required.
